CYENCE – Czech Youth Expo & Conference Proposal · 3 October 2026

Logo manual

This proposal follows the logo system of Zvaž vědu! and Generace vesmír: a framed rectangle with an icon on the left and the name on the right. The icon is the CYENCE interference pattern itself. It is a proposal to discuss with the identity designer; nothing here is final.

01

The icon is a frozen wave

Two sources, one yellow and one blue, each send out circular waves. A cell on a yellow crest is yellow, on a blue crest blue, on both black, and on neither it stays cream. The icon is one fixed 12 × 12 frame of this interference, the same rule that draws every pattern on the website.

02

Construction

Everything sits on one grid. The unit x is the thickness of the frame, and every other measure is a whole number of x: the icon, the wordmark and the margins all snap to the same cells.

Logo
36 × 14 x (2.57 : 1)
Frame
1 x on all sides
Icon
12 × 12 x, flush with the frame
Wordmark
ZV! Display, cap height 5 x, 2 x from the frame

Keep a clear space of at least 2 x around the logo (yellow above). No text, image edge or other logo may enter it.

03

Versions

Use the primary colour logo wherever colour is available. The black & white version is for one-colour print. In one colour the two waves cannot be told apart and their interference would read as random squares, so it shows the yellow wave alone: the same tile, the same rings, one colour.

04

Backgrounds

The logo is a closed rectangle, so the colour version holds on every brand colour. On black or dark photos switch to the negative.

05

Colour & type

The logo uses only the four CYENCE colours. The wordmark is ZV! Display in capitals; it is part of the artwork, so always use the files rather than typing the name. Print values (CMYK, Pantone) are to be confirmed with the designer.

06

Minimum size

Below these sizes the pattern turns into noise. Use the icon on its own where the full logo would be smaller.

07

Don’t

The logo works because every part is fixed to the grid. Keep it whole.

08
