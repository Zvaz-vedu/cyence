  - type: intro

    title: "Where young researchers meet"
    text: |
      CYENCE is an international science competition and conference that brings together talented young researchers and changemakers from around the world. It gives them a unique opportunity to present their own research projects, measure their results in an international competition, share experience and build connections with young scientists across countries and fields.

      During a week-long programme in Brno, participants present their research to an international expert jury, get to know leading Czech universities and research institutions, and take part in a varied programme of academic, cultural and social activities. CYENCE is a space not only for a science competition, but also for international cooperation, inspiration and lasting friendships.

      A unique part of the event is the **CYENCE Roadshow**: the international participants travel to multiple cities across the Czech Republic and present their research to thousands of Czech high school students. They bring them the latest scientific findings, share their own experience with research and inspire them to explore the world of science while still at secondary school.

      The event is organised by **Zvaž vědu!, z. s.** and the **Brno Observatory and Planetarium** under the auspices of the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports of the Czech Republic.
