    text: |
      The CYENCE Roadshow is a unique part of the whole event. It brings the international science competition straight to Czech students and gives them the chance to meet talented young researchers of their own generation, learn about their work and find out how they can get involved in science themselves.

      The Roadshow builds on the proven format of the student organisation Zvaž vědu!, which gives young scientists space to present their own research and inspire their peers to pursue science. This concept now goes international: instead of Czech speakers, young scientists from all over the world come to share their research, experience and passion for science with Czech high school students.

      Zvaž vědu! is a Czech student-led non-profit founded in 2023 to popularise science and technology and to open up new opportunities in research for high school students. Since then it has grown into a nationwide initiative that reaches thousands of students across the Czech Republic through science conferences, talks, workshops, excursions and educational programmes. The CYENCE Roadshow builds on this proven concept and takes it international for the first time.
